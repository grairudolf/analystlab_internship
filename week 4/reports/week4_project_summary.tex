\section*{1. Specific Problem Addressed}
HealthConnect Clinic experiences significant operational disruptions and unrecoverable idle capacity caused by patient appointment no-shows ($48.46\%$ baseline non-attendance rate across 5,000 appointment records). The Machine Learning track addresses this problem by developing an automated prediction and decision engine to identify high-risk non-attendance prior to scheduled appointment times, enabling proactive multi-channel interventions and schedule optimization.

\section*{2. Resources Utilized}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{\texttt{HealthConnect\_Appointment\_Data.csv}:} $5,000$ appointment records across $1,696$ unique patients, containing demographic, scheduling, historical attendance, and reminder metrics.
    \item \textbf{\texttt{HealthConnect\_Data\_Dictionary.xlsx}:} Complete field definition dictionary specifying data types, role constraints, and descriptions.
    \item \textbf{\texttt{HealthConnect\_Clinic\_Knowledge\_Base.docx}:} Clinic operational rules, cancellation policies, and administrative procedures.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{3. Key Observations from Week 4}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Target Distribution:} No-Show ($48.46\%$, $N=2,423$), Attended ($46.28\%$, $N=2,314$), Cancelled ($5.26\%$, $N=263$).
    \item \textbf{Booking Lead Time Effect:} Appointments booked $\ge 30$ days in advance exhibit over $65\%$ no-show rates, compared to $<25\%$ for appointments booked within 3 days.
    \item \textbf{Historical Recidivism:} Patients with $\ge 2$ previous no-shows exhibit a $>75\%$ probability of missing subsequent appointments.
    \item \textbf{Data Quality:} \texttt{reminder\_channel} missingness ($27.32\%$) is NMAR (corresponds to no reminder sent); \texttt{waiting\_time\_minutes} presents a critical target leakage risk and must be excluded.
\end{itemize}

\section*{4. Proposed Technical \& System Approach}
We propose a supervised binary classification model $P(\text{No-Show} \mid X)$ integrated into a 5-stage production architecture: Data Ingestion $\rightarrow$ Validation \& Feature Store $\rightarrow$ Inference Microservice $\rightarrow$ Actionable Decision Engine $\rightarrow$ MLOps Logging. Patients are stratified into High, Moderate, and Low risk tiers to trigger automated WhatsApp/SMS confirmation workflows.

\section*{5. Key Considerations, Risks \& Constraints}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Data Leakage Boundary:} Explicit exclusion of post-arrival variables (\texttt{waiting\_time\_minutes}).
    \item \textbf{Patient Clustering:} GroupKFold cross-validation grouped by \texttt{patient\_id} to prevent temporal behavior leakage across multiple patient appointments.
    \item \textbf{Ethical Compliance:} Ensuring equitable prediction accuracy across age and gender subgroups without service discrimination.
\end{itemize}

\section*{6. Proposed Focus for Week 5}
In Week 5, work will progress to \textbf{Data Preprocessing, Feature Engineering \& Baseline Model Prototyping}:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Constructing automated scikit-learn preprocessing and imputation pipelines.
    \item Engineering behavioral features (\texttt{historical\_noshow\_ratio}, lead time interactions).
    \item Benchmarking baseline classification models (Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, Random Forest, XGBoost, LightGBM).
    \item Tuning classification decision thresholds for high recall performance.
\end{enumerate}
